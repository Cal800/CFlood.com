Research Report

This was the first draft of my UI screens for CollabNoteZ and my first experience with professional wireframing tools. The report focuses on explaining functionality for each screen and the overall application of CollabnoteZ along with applications that I took Inspiration from. These apps inspired some of the features of CollabNoteZ and aided it to become an all in one application. Lastly a persona was developed that outlined how a typical user would use our product and how they would benefit.

These flow charts were designed and created using Balsamiq Cloud

The Final Wireframes

The essence of this project was always about showing improvement through iteration and taking feedback and refining your designs. These wireframes are a step up in fidelity as we use colour, themes and icons. I went for a modern liquid glass look and a streamlined design. The design updates emphasize a clear leap in execution. By shifting away from raw, monochrome sketches and embracing a structured palette, the interface translates user feedback into real, practical usability upgrades.

These high fidelity mockups were designed and created using the industry standard tool. Figma

Project Outcome

This project opened my eyes to the realm of UI/UX Design as a secondary field of interest for me; it deepened my knowledge of User Interface design software such as Balsamiq & the industry standard Figma, and bolstered my skills in creative design and writing documentation. This project served as an excellent entry to UI/UX study and cemented my interest in the field due to its creative and innovative qualities.
