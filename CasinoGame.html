Baccarat

Project Details

A fun, casino-style game of Baccarat built in Java. Pick Player, Banker or Tie, place your chips and watch the table come to life in ASCII, with a chip balance that rises and falls so every bet feels like it counts. It’s made to look good, play smoothly and capture the feel of a real casino table.

Designed, written and debugged in a single day with minimal AI assistance, so the scope is deliberately lean and the code is all mine.

System Instructions

To interact with the terminal, type the Number 1 (or Play!) and hit enter to enter the casino table, or type 2 to view the game rules. Once seated, you will be prompted to select your starting chip balance before each round begins. From there, choose your predicted outcome between Player, Banker, or Tie, and place a valid wager in increments of 500. The dealer will then deal the cards, evaluate the scores automatically, and display your updated chip balance based on the winning hand. After a round you can choose to play again or quit.

This terminal serves as a front-end simulation of the core functions and logic. For the original Java version, you can find it on my Github.
