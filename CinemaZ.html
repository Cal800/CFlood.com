Why User Flowcharts

In UI/UX design, user flow is incredibly important as it highlights priority screens and helps designers map out common tasks to gain a better understanding of the app's functions and features. Flowcharts allow us to decrease travel time between menus and handle errors; they are a vital tool in the world of UI/UX design.

These flowcharts were designed and created using LucidChart

The Blueprint

Each of these wireframes was designed with UI principles and security in mind from step 1. The goal was not only to build a convenient app that could be used by anyone, but also an ecosystem for movie fanatics. The movie membership tab and Letterboxd-style movie list bring a new angle of use to the application that stays relevant to the brand. Common UI principles that were always considered were consistency, recognition over recall & reducing friction. CinemaZ was designed to be a hub for casual and dedicated movie enthusiasts that focused on UI/UX design at every step of the way.

These high-fidelity wireframes were designed and created using Mockflow
