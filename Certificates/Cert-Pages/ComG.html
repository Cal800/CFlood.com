Summary of the Course

This course provided an introduction to the importance of communication. It focused on the principles that veered away from just conveying points and leaned towards creating material that instead prioritised content, engagement and captivation. The module explored methodologies utilized at Google to design streamlined, visually compelling presentations that not only convey points and ideas, but stay memorable.

Learning Objectives

The primary focus of this course was centered around actionable storytelling & design workflows that emphasized engagement, the core objectives were:

  • Analyse current presentation & storytelling methods and identify problems relating to engagement and memorability.
  • Explore methods of idea conveying that focus on a visual aspect such as mindmaps and storyboards.
  • Optimise slide creation and speaking skills to help deliver more impactful presentation
  • Translate complex data through the use of visual content,narrative structuring, and dynamic delivery adjustments.

Work Produced & Problems Overcome

Communicating your ideas visually

In this course I assumed the role of an advisor within my software team, My role being centered around giving advice to my fellow team members on presentation technique, The module began by discussing methods used to bring points to life using a visual approach, It focused on remaining in the planning stages and explored techniques like mindmaps and storyboards, and how they can be utilised to develop and expand on ideas, Mindmaps use sub-topics and relation theory to group scattered business topics & blueprint ideas. While storyboards map out a sequential, panel-by-panel timeline to convey complex narratives and information in a highly structured, visual way. Finally I was asked to advise some of my team members on the best use cases for storyboards & mindmaps and how each one can be used to capture their work in an engaging and visually appealing format.

Graphic Design Focused on Engagement

This section of the module focused on communication through slides, exploring how to design and format presentations to be both informative and visually appealing. This task saw me dive into presentation layouts and alignment grids, learning to identify design anti-patterns like overwhelming text blocks and a lack of contextual imagery. Additionally, I focused on typography, visual hierarchy, branding consistency, and utilizing white space effectively to maximize structural clarity. Lastly, I applied these principles by advising a team member on a poorly formatted slide deck. After auditing their layout and identifying cluttered text blocks, I provided actionable guidance to optimize their slide design, steering them toward a clean, high-impact layout with clear, scannable points.

Engaging with your Audience

Lastly within this course, we focused on presentation techniques, as a clear and confident speaker is just as vital as visually appealing slides are. This section explored the "Start with Why" framework, and why establishing a clear objective elevates your presentation and keeps it centered on a topic or idea rather than data dumping. It discussed techniques used to keep a steady pace while presenting, such as PechaKucha, focusing on delivering clear and relevant information that captivated the audience. I looked at the presentation mindset and focusing on audience empathy, identifying text-heavy slides, and using captivating images. Finally, I was given a full topic quiz with the purpose being to cement the ideas of a steady pace, a memorable presence, and an engaging performance.

To further apply and cement my understanding, I created a custom infographic using Canva. This deliverable emphasizes the core communication principles and design techniques taught throughout the course, strictly adhering to the exact alignment, hierarchy, and storytelling methodologies modeled in the training.
