Summary of the Course

This course provided insight into the work carried out by cybersecurity consulting firms in the form of risk management and incident response. The course focused on responding to cyber-based incidents and recording them with the mindset of an auditor. The module also had me assume the role of a head security analyst, tasked with evaluating a business's technological framework and providing a risk assessment alongside strategic mitigation advice. The course was designed to showcase IT auditing with a particular focus on risk.

Learning Objectives

The primary focus of this course was based on the work completed by Security Consulting Firms, those were:

  • Respond to security incidents with an analytical mindset and accurately reconstruct the events that led to the breach.
  • Document technical vulnerabilities and map incident timelines while utilizing industry-standard security frameworks.
  • Perform a comprehensive risk assessment on a company's technological infrastructure and provide actionable mitigation advice.
  • Deepen knowledge of IT auditing and risk scoring by identifying, analyzing, and prioritizing potential threats.

Work Produced & Problems Overcome

Task 1

For Task 1, I assumed the role of an incident responder, responsible for handling a live security incident inside a business that resulted in encrypted servers and a potential ransom. First, I determined how the attacker gained initial access to the system by establishing an incident timeline to map the entry method and subsequent events. Tracing an attacker's movements is a core practice within incident response, following the industry-standard lifecycle of find, contain, eradicate, record. Next, I prepared an assessment of all affected systems and compromised data to map the potential damages and plan our next steps. Documenting the signs of compromise was the next critical step. Identifying how the attackers breached the network and noting the warning signs that went undetected helped us better understand our security vulnerabilities. Lastly, I completed a business impact analysis to evaluate the incident's impact on both financial and operational capacities. Together, this comprehensive report served as a complete breakdown of the security incident, designed to strengthen an analytical mindset and the ability to document complex incidents in a scannable, high-value format.

Task 2

Task 2 saw a direct shift away from incident response towards formal IT auditing. IT auditing is a staple in security consulting companies. It was my responsibility to evaluate a business's complete technological architecture, from databases and financial systems to employee access and HR. As an auditor, I had to view the entire framework in regards to risk. Risk is the largest factor in security, and managing risk properly separates advanced security systems that combine human factors from systems that cost businesses millions. Using Canva, I created my own industry-standard, full security risk assessment that focused on analysing 3 potential errors within the business's architectural framework. The evaluation of potential vulnerabilities, existing controls, and associated threats aided in calculating an overall risk score to better offer mitigation advice & Post-Risk Analysis. I was tasked with covering the business's system architecture visually, explaining the importance of risk and how it was calculated, before designing and documenting three full risk analyses. This task was fully based on IT auditing and was designed to highlight the importance of risk and drive home key concepts and best practices.
